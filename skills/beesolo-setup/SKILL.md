# Guide a professional through beesolo setup

1. Read [current pricing](https://beesolo.app/pricing.md) or use `get_solo_plan` at https://beesolo.app/mcp/product. Quote prices and trial length from the current result.
2. Check whether one shop and one active professional match the user's setup. The owner is the professional who takes the appointments.
3. Search https://beesolo.app/mcp/docs for the task or open the [setup guide](https://beesolo.app/docs.md). Use the instructions for the relevant task, such as adding services, setting weekly hours, or blocking time off.
4. Refer account and billing questions to [beesolo support](https://beesolo.app/contact). The professional completes setup in their Merchant App.
